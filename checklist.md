# How to Spot a Phishing Email - Checklist

| # | Red flag | What to look for | Example |
|---|----------|------------------|---------|
| 1 | Urgent or threatening language | "Act now", "account suspended", "within 24 hours" | email1, email2 |
| 2 | Mismatched links | Link text says one site, hover shows another | email1, email3 |
| 3 | Unknown / look-alike sender | `paypa1`, `amaz0n`, odd endings like .xyz/.top | email1, email3 |
| 4 | Requests for sensitive info | Passwords, card numbers, OTPs by email | email1, email2 |
| 5 | Generic greeting | "Dear customer" instead of your name | email1, email2 |
| 6 | Unexpected attachments | .zip/.exe or surprise invoices | email3 |
| 7 | Too-good-to-be-true offers | Free prizes, gift cards | email2 |

**Safe habit:** never click - open the official website yourself, and report the email.
